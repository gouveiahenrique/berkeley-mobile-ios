ASUC Senate Open Forum — Fall 2026
Thursday, October 29
12:00 AM All Day
MLK Student Union, Pauley Ballroom
Description
The ASUC Senate holds an open forum for all UC Berkeley students to engage directly with elected senators. Topics include dining hall contract negotiations, campus housing policy, student fee allocations for the 2026–27 academic year, and community safety initiatives. All registered students are welcome to attend and ask questions during the public comment period.
