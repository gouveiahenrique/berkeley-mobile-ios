Cesar Chavez Day – Campus Holiday
Thursday, April 16, 2026
All Day 12:00 AM
UC Berkeley Campus
Description
In honor of Cesar Chavez Day, UC Berkeley will observe a campus-wide holiday. Administrative offices, most libraries, and many campus services will be closed. The Recreational Sports Facility will maintain reduced hours. Students are encouraged to reflect on Cesar Chavez's legacy of labor rights, civil rights, and social justice.
